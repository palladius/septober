# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style: Tone and Voice

The tone and voice of this application and all related communications should strike a balance between being friendly, direct, and having a touch of humor.

- **Friendly and Approachable:** The language should be conversational and encouraging. We want users to feel comfortable and empowered, not intimidated by the tool.
- **Direct and Concise:** Especially in the CLI, communication should be clear, to the point, and free of unnecessary jargon.
- **A Touch of Humor:** Where appropriate, a bit of wit can make the user experience more enjoyable. This should be used sparingly and in a way that doesn't detract from the clarity of the communication.

The overall goal is to create a tool that is both highly functional and a pleasure to use.
